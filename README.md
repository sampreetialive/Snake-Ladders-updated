#Snake & Ladders Game:

-> Setup and Start
Place all player pieces on the starting square, usually number 1.
Roll a die to see who goes first; the player with the highest number takes the first turn.
Take turns moving your piece forward along the numbered grid. 

-> Ladders and Snakes
Ladders: If your piece lands on the bottom of a ladder, you climb up to the top square.
Snakes: If your piece lands on the head of a snake, you slide down to the tail square. 

-> Winning the Game
You must land exact on the final square (100) to win.
If your die roll is higher than the remaining spaces needed to reach 100, you either stay in place or bounce backward depending on house rules.
